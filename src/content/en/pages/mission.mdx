## Our vision

A democratic public sphere in which the dignity of lived experience is protected, people can participate meaningfully, and institutions remain answerable for their actions.

Res Publica understands the *res publica* as a shared public matter. It belongs to no single institution, technical platform or voice. It takes form when people listen, review knowledge, share responsibility and change conditions together.

## Our mission

We strengthen the democratic public sphere through three connected fields:

1. **Research** — independent inquiry, traceable methods and an honest treatment of uncertainty.
2. **Dialogue** — structured spaces in which experience is heard without erasing difference.
3. **Participation** — practical paths through which people and communities can contribute over time to public life and accountable institution-building.

## Two connected movements

At the human level, the path moves from **lived experience** through **being heard** and **meaning** to **agency**.

At the institutional level, it moves from **documentation** through **evidence** and **responsibility** to **repair**.

The movements belong together. Experience without an accountable institutional response can remain isolated. Institutional action without lived experience loses orientation.

## Our measure

Attention alone is not a measure of democratic effect. What matters is whether participation becomes possible, decisions become better informed, institutions become more answerable, and recognised harm can lead to learning and repair.
