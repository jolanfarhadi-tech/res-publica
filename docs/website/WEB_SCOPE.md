# Res Publica Website Scope

## Authority and source mapping

This scope is governed by the approved accountability Constitution at
`brain/00_constitution/00_constitution.md`. The formerly referenced standalone
Vision and Mission files are not present under `docs/source/`; their current
canonical public equivalent is `docs/source/foundation/00_MANIFESTO.md`. It
states the founding purpose, the north star, and the three public pillars of
research, dialogue, and participation. `brain/VISION.md` and `brain/MISSION.md`
remain secondary cross-checks only.

## In scope

- A public, trilingual orientation and communication surface for Res Publica.
- Calm institutional brand expression: wordmark, a non-gamified symbolic mark,
  typography, colour, and public-page composition.
- Public explanation of purpose, research, dialogue, participation, civic
  learning, membership, projects, publications, and public events where the
  underlying content is verified for publication.
- Clear, bounded entry points to public contact, membership application, and
  existing authentication without overstating their status or opening private
  data.
- Accessibility, DE/EN/FA localisation, Persian RTL, provenance, and the
  existing public/private boundary.

## Out of scope

- Replacing the constitutional domain model, accepted ADRs, APIs, persistence,
  authentication, or authorization rules.
- Presenting internal systems, operational controls, the Executive AI Office,
  audit infrastructure, or Publishing Authority workflows as public products.
- Public access to protected records, profiles, dashboards, administrative
  operations, hearings, governance assignments, or Publishing Authority write
  capabilities.
- Invented people, partnerships, projects, impact figures, testimonials,
  claims, or operational availability.
- Engagement scores, ranks, badges, progress mechanics, or attention-based
  success measures.

## Relationship to authenticated surfaces

The repository contains protected Member Profile, Dashboard, Operations, and
other platform routes. They remain functioning private surfaces, but they are
not part of the public narrative or the logo/front-page redesign. Their
authorization, MFA, consent, audit, and persistence boundaries are preserved.

## Audiences

- **Participants and individuals:** understand a dignified path from lived
  experience toward being heard, shared meaning, and agency.
- **Communities:** understand how sustained dialogue and civic fellowship can
  build shared capacity without ranking people.
- **Researchers:** find methods, sources, uncertainty, and publication
  boundaries that support accountable work.
- **Institutions and media:** understand the relationship between
  documentation, evidence, responsibility, repair, and public answerability.

## Qualitative success criteria

- A visitor can recognise Res Publica as a calm, credible civic institution,
  not a commercial platform or campaign.
- The homepage makes both the human and institutional journeys intelligible
  without reducing either to a technical workflow.
- The symbolic mark communicates orientation and relation, never evidence
  strength, membership standing, or rank.
- Every public claim remains traceable to approved repository material or is
  withheld.
- DE, EN, and FA are treated as complete public experiences; Persian remains
  naturally RTL and no language becomes a visual afterthought.

## Dependencies and safeguards

This public layer depends on the established sitemap, content-model,
internationalisation, legal/form, accessibility/SEO, deployment, and
drift-check work. It must continue to respect the applicable DPIA and privacy
gates. The brand and homepage may guide orientation, but never bypass the
constitutional, authorization, consent, provenance, or publication controls
behind the platform.
