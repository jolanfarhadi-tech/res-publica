# Frontend Design Rationale

## Institutional premise

Res Publica is presented as a civic institution joining research, dialogue,
participation, democratic governance, public accountability, evidence, and
education. The signed Satzung is the primary identity source. It supports
charitable political education, international understanding and intercultural
dialogue, digital governance, democratic innovation, civic
self-organisation, and cooperation with science, public institutions, and
civil society.

The design therefore aims for calm authority rather than promotion. Large
editorial type establishes public purpose; grids and observatory circles evoke
inquiry and institutional memory; translucent control surfaces show bounded
layers without turning the site into a software dashboard.

## Narrative structure

The homepage carries the complete public sequence:

1. public purpose and democratic capacity;
2. mission grounded in the Satzung;
3. research as a way to make questions examinable;
4. projects as bounded, sourced work;
5. programmes as sustained civic learning;
6. the Lab as a research and innovation environment;
7. publications, events, and news as provenance-gated public records;
8. membership as an accountable relationship;
9. cooperation only when confirmed;
10. a restrained invitation to participate or make contact.

This preserves the dual journey:

- human: experience → being heard → meaning → agency;
- institutional: documentation → evidence → responsibility → repair.

## Product and methodology boundaries

Products, programmes, services, projects, and methods remain distinct.
Products list only implemented digital interfaces. Programmes describe RPCS /
Civic School and Civic Fellowship at their verified maturity. HARM appears
under Projects as research and methodology in development. Internal
infrastructure and Publishing Authority are not marketed as public offerings.

## Trust and privacy

Trust is expressed through provenance, bounded authority, truthful empty
states, explicit consent, private-by-default profiles, and clear decision
responsibility. The interface does not simulate successful contact delivery,
invent social proof, or imply that technical readiness equals publication.

The preference system is deliberately local and conservative: optional
categories begin disabled, necessary storage cannot be disabled, and no
analytics provider is added by the UI itself.

## Accessibility and internationalization

The system is designed once for DE, EN, and FA rather than translated after
layout. Logical CSS properties, native Persian typography, RTL composition,
localized dates, resilient motion, keyboard controls, and accessible status
messaging are core constraints, not finishing passes.
