# Terms and service-rules needs assessment — legal-review draft

**Not approved for publication. Not legal advice.**

## Likely distinct instruments

- The association's signed Satzung remains the authority for membership,
  admission and institutional governance. Website copy must not replace it.
- A short account-use policy may be needed for authenticated access, credential
  security, acceptable use, suspension, and service availability.
- Event participation rules may be needed only when real events and operating
  conditions are approved.
- Programme/Civic School participation rules are premature until staffing,
  dates, selection, attendance and completion rules are operationally
  approved.
- Community moderation rules are mandatory before any open posting capability
  is activated.
- Publishing contributor/reviewer terms may be needed for rights, provenance,
  confidentiality and withdrawal, but must preserve Publishing Authority
  separation of duties and the no-auto-publish boundary.

## Owner/legal questions

1. Which services create a contractual relationship beyond association
   membership?
2. Which rules belong in the Satzung, a board policy, participant agreement,
   consent notice, or public terms?
3. What age, jurisdiction, safeguarding, accessibility, complaint, termination
   and dispute provisions apply?
4. What content licenses or confidentiality duties apply to research,
   Publishing, HARM and community contributions?
5. Which translations require certified legal equivalence, and which language
   controls if versions diverge?

No terms should be generated from generic templates or published until these
questions are answered and approved.
