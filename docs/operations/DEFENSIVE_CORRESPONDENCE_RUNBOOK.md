# Defensive correspondence operations runbook

This runbook governs the bounded A→A′ ledger. It does not authorize hack-back,
provider changes, secret rotation, account isolation, Production migration or
real Research activation.

## Intake and evaluation

1. Open a technical incident under the existing exact-scope, recent-MFA flow.
2. Preserve privacy-minimized observation evidence before creating loop events.
3. Record only ordered Loops 1–5 with exact incident, target and evidence IDs.
4. Treat E0/E1 results as reversible observation or inert synthetic branch
   state. Do not describe them as containment.
5. For E2/E3, appoint an independent reviewer. The incident opener and
   evaluator cannot approve the response.

## Review

The reviewer verifies evidence independence, contradictions, target scope,
impact class and reversibility. Approval of `ALERT_OPERATOR` or
`PREPARE_QUARANTINE` records only that bounded internal effect. Actual session,
token, account, capability, provider, secret, database, Research or recovery
changes must use their existing separate controls and approval procedures.

## Effect and rollback

Verify the protected Security Operations overview contains the exact policy,
evidence level, target, effect transitions and hashes. A rollback may be
recorded only after `EFFECT_VERIFIED`. Confirm unrelated public and protected
services remain available. Never delete or edit the original signal, decision
or transition; append a corrective incident record if evidence was wrong.

## Self-DoS checks

Before expanding any autonomous power, rerun synthetic tests for fake Research
probing, fake RAG attacks, fake administrator attacks, distributed low-
confidence events, repeated decoy interaction, replay, reordering and
contradictory evidence. All must remain at Class 0/1 or require independent
review. No attacker-derived sequence may become permanent policy.

## External escalation

Class 4 structural actions and real provider effects require the corresponding
owner/legal/dual-control gate. Use the capability-quarantine, incident-response
and clean-recovery runbooks. Do not translate an internal preparation record
into a claim that Auth0, Vercel, Neon or any external provider changed.
